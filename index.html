01About

I'm Huzaifa, a web developer and CRM specialist from Pakistan. Since 2020 I have been building websites, online stores and web apps for clients, and now the GoHighLevel pipelines that make sure every lead they bring in gets an answer.

I started freelancing with WordPress, writing custom themes and plugins in PHP. Graphic design came next, then mobile apps with Flutter, and now React and React Native.

For a US business-funding company I built the website, the application form and the route every lead takes into GoHighLevel: tagged by source, filtered, and in front of sales in under a minute.

Between client projects I build my own products: a motion graphics editor, a multiplayer browser game, a queue app for small businesses and a radar that finds remote work for my team.

03Services

What I can do for you

  • GoHighLevel set up and looked after: pipelines, lead routing, form-to-CRM webhooks and the texts and emails that go out on their own, all tested before anything goes live.

  • Fast first contact on inbound leads, follow-up by phone, text and email, booked calendars for your sales team, and a weekly report on bookings and show rate.

  • Custom themes and plugins written in clean PHP, or a careful build in Elementor. Fast to load and easy for you to edit after I hand it over.

  • Layouts designed in Figma around your brand and your visitors, so the site looks right and also brings in enquiries. Every page is designed for phones first.

  • Shops on Shopify or WordPress with products, payments and checkout set up and tested, plus the content uploaded so you can start selling straight away.

  • Clear, friendly app screens, designed so they can be built in Flutter or React Native without surprises. I stay with the project until it is ready to launch.

  • A logo, type, colours and simple guidelines that hold together everywhere, and motion graphics that explain what you do in a few seconds.

  • On-page SEO, speed work and tidy social profiles, so people who are already searching for what you sell can find you.
