02Case studyClient work, 2026

From the form to the sales desk in under a minute

A US business-funding company. I built its website and application form, run its lead campaigns, and built the route every lead takes into GoHighLevel. The company's name is shared on request.

The problem

The sales team only works leads that arrive in GoHighLevel. Leads from a new Facebook form were sitting inside Meta, where nobody would ever call them. A lead nobody calls is a lead paid for and lost.

What I did

I built a route from every lead source into one workflow, with the source tagged, test and junk leads branched off, and the good ones turned into an opportunity and an email to sales. Then I proved it with test leads before the ads went back on.

What I built

  • Lead form to CRM bridge. Facebook lead form to a sheet, a script that syncs every minute, and a GoHighLevel inbound webhook.
  • One workflow with branches. Test leads stop, leads under the client's minimum are filtered, qualified leads become an opportunity and an email to sales.
  • Website form into the CRM. A seven-step application form I wrote as a WordPress plugin, creating the contact through the GoHighLevel API.

What the team got

  • Source on every contact. Meta, Google Ads or the website, as tags the team can filter by.
  • Conversion tracking. Google Tag Manager with two conversions: application started and application submitted.
  • Lead quality, measured. Leads read one by one for revenue, time in business and credit, and reported to the owner every week.

Nothing goes live until a test lead has landed in the CRM.

The rule I work by since this project. The first live lead after the fix went through to the sales team's pipeline and inbox the next day.
