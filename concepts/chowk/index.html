Tawa to gate
in four moves

No app to download and no account to make. Order here, pay at the door, eat while it is still too hot.

  1. Pick your food

    Tap Add. Keep tapping. Nobody here is counting.

  2. Tell us where

    Write the address the way you would say it to a rider. “Black gate after the tandoor” works fine.

  3. We light the tawa

    Nothing is cooked until you order. That is why it takes 30 minutes and not 5.

  4. Listen for the honk

    Pay by cash, card or wallet at the gate. If the chai is not hot, we make another.
