Why Raag

Built for music that takes its time.

A khayal can run for an hour. A qawwali builds for twenty minutes before it lets go. Raag is made for listening like that, start to finish, with nothing rushing you to the next track.

Listen by the hour

Every raga belongs to a part of the day. Raag reads your clock and plays dawn ragas at dawn, monsoon ragas when it rains and night ragas after the house goes quiet.
