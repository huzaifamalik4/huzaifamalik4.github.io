How a trip runs

From your sofa to the first camp.

You bring the legs. We look after the jeeps, the permits, the cooking and the weather calls.

  1. 01

    Pick a date

    Choose a trek and a departure. A deposit of PKR 15,000 holds your place and is refundable until 30 days before.

  2. 02

    Get ready

    We send a kit list and a four-week walking plan. Your lead guide calls to answer whatever the list did not.

  3. 03

    Meet in the north

    We collect you in Gilgit or Skardu, from the airport or the bus stand. Jeeps, permits and porters are already arranged.

  4. 04

    Walk

    Five to seven hours a day at the pace of the slowest walker, with rest days built in as the camps get higher.

What you can count on

Small groups, local guides, one price.

The same three rules on every departure, from the five-day walk to the sixteen-day one.

  • 8walkers at most

    Small groups keep the camps quiet and the pace kind. A trek runs with as few as four.

  • 2guides on every trek

    A lead and a sweep, both from Gilgit-Baltistan and both trained in mountain first aid. Nobody walks last alone.

  • 1price, nothing extra on the trail

    Tents, meals, jeeps, porters, permits and park fees are all in it. You bring boots, a sleeping bag and a day pack.

In the price

  • Jeeps from the meeting point and back
  • Two-person tents, mats and a mess tent
  • Three cooked meals a day and boiled water
  • Porters or mules for 12 kg of your kit
  • Trekking permits and national park fees
  • First aid kit and a satellite messenger with the lead guide

Not in the price

  • Travel to Gilgit or Skardu
  • Sleeping bag and boots (both can be hired)
  • Travel insurance
  • Tips for the crew
